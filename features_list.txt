FEATURES MEASURED BY features.py
27 features per image, from the BSE image only. Sizes are in pixels.

COMPOSITION (4)
  pore_frac             Share of the image that is dark (pores and cracks)
  bulk_frac             Share that is the grey bulk material
  bright_frac           Share that is bright particles
  bright_bulk_ratio     Brightness of the bright particles divided by brightness of the bulk

BRIGHT PARTICLES (6)
  bright_per_mpx        Number of bright particles per million pixels
  bright_diam_mean      Average particle diameter
  bright_diam_cv        Spread of particle sizes relative to the average
  bright_diam_max       Diameter of the largest particle
  bright_solidity       How compact the particles are (1 = no dents or jagged edges)
  bright_aspect         Length divided by width (1 = round, higher = elongated)

BRIGHT PARTICLE SPACING (3)
  bright_nn_dist        Average distance from each particle to its nearest neighbour
  bright_clustering     That distance compared with random scatter (1 = random, below 1 = clumped)
  bright_patchiness     How much the bright fraction varies across six vertical strips of the image

PORES (6)
  big_void_frac         Area share of large gaps between particles
  crack_frac            Area share of long thin dark features
  round_pore_frac       Area share of compact dark features
  pores_per_mpx         Number of cracks and round pores per million pixels
  pore_chord_h          Average pore width measured horizontally
  pore_chord_v          Average pore width measured vertically

DIRECTION (3)
  pore_anisotropy       Horizontal pore width divided by vertical (above 1 = pores lie flat)
  pore_verticality      Average pore orientation (1 = vertical, 0 = horizontal)
  bulk_chord_ratio      The same horizontal-to-vertical ratio for the bulk material

INTERFACES (3)
  pore_solid_boundary   Amount of boundary between pore and solid
  bright_bulk_boundary  Amount of boundary between bright particles and bulk
  bright_pore_contact   Share of the bright particles' surface that faces a pore

SPATIAL STATISTICS (2)
  pore_corr_length      Typical pore feature scale, from ImageRep
  bright_corr_length    Typical bright feature scale, from ImageRep

EXTRA COLUMNS IN features.csv (not used as features)
  _pore_frac_abs_err    ImageRep error bar on the pore fraction for that single image
  _bright_frac_abs_err  ImageRep error bar on the bright fraction for that single image

SETTINGS THAT AFFECT THESE (top of features.py)
  SIGMA = 1.5            Smoothing before thresholding
  MIN_BRIGHT_PX = 150    Bright specks smaller than this are ignored
  MIN_FEATURE_PX = 20    Dark specks smaller than this are ignored
  BIG_VOID_FRAC = 0.005  Dark region above this share of the image counts as a gap between particles
  CRACK_ASPECT = 3.0     Length/width above this counts as a crack
